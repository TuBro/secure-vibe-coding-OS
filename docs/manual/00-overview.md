# Secure Vibe Coding OS — Operations Manual

**Version:** 1.0  
**Organisation:** Polypus Feke Group  
**Maintained by:** Operations Team  

---

## What Is HarperOS?

HarperOS (Secure Vibe Coding OS) is the internal operating system for Polypus Feke Group. It is a real-time business dashboard that centralises sales, communications, marketing, support, and social media management into a single platform.

Everything is live. There is no save button. Data appears the moment it is entered.

---

## Who Is This Manual For?

This manual is for new team members joining Polypus Feke Group who need to operate HarperOS from day one. No technical background is required.

---

## How To Access HarperOS

1. Open your browser
2. Go to: `https://secure-vibe-coding-os-three.vercel.app`
3. Sign in with your team email via Clerk authentication
4. You will land on the **Dashboard**

---

## The Dashboard — What You See

From top to bottom the dashboard has the following sections:

| # | Section | Purpose |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | **KPI Cards** | Live counts — Active Leads, New Today, Voice Messages, Unread Messages |
| 2 | **Voice Activity Feed** | Transcribed Telegram voice notes from the team |
| 3 | **Task Board** | Team to-do list — To Do / In Progress / Done |
| 4 | **Marketing Engine** | Campaign management with AI brief generation |
| 5 | **Sales Pipeline** | Lead tracking Kanban board |
| 6 | **Communications Hub** | Unified outreach log across all channels |
| 7 | **Support Tickets** | Client and internal issue tracking |
| 8 | **Social Calendar** | Content queue and bookings/meetings |
| 9 | **System** | Token usage and platform health |

## The Telegram Bot — Te Ra

Te Ra is the HarperOS voice assistant on Telegram.

- Send her a **voice note** on Telegram
- She **transcribes** it using AI and saves it to the dashboard
- She **talks back** with a voice reply
- All notes appear in the **Voice Activity Feed** in real time

Te Ra is always listening when the bridge is running.

## Why We Start Manual Before We Automate

Every section of this system can eventually be automated. Leads can be created automatically from Telegram messages. Tickets can be raised from client messages without anyone touching a button. Campaigns can be triggered on a schedule.

But we start manual — on purpose.

**Here is why:**

1. **You need to understand the system before you trust it** — automation that you don't understand is a liability, not an asset
2. **Manual use reveals what to automate** — after a week of manually moving leads across the pipeline, you will know exactly which steps are repetitive and where automation will save the most time
3. **Data quality comes from humans first** — when a person enters a lead, they make judgement calls a machine cannot. That judgement shapes the rules automation will later follow
4. **If automation breaks, you can run the system by hand** — teams that only know the automated version are helpless when something goes wrong

Learn the manual process first. Master it. Then we automate the parts that don't need human judgement.

---

## Golden Rules

1. **Everything saves automatically** — no save button, no confirmation needed
2. **Data is live** — what you enter is visible to the whole team instantly
3. **Telegram is your mobile input** — use voice notes on the go, check the dashboard at your desk
4. **Status badges are clickable** — click any status to move it to the next stage
5. **Trash icon deletes** — deletions are permanent, confirm before clicking
