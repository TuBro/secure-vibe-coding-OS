# 03 — Marketing Engine

**Section:** Marketing  
**Tool:** Campaign Manager with AI Brief Generation  
**Location:** Dashboard → scroll to Marketing Engine

---

> **Remember:** You are doing this manually first so you understand it fully. Once you can run this step without thinking, that is when we automate it.

---

## Purpose

The Marketing Engine is where you plan, track, and execute campaigns across the business.

Every campaign — whether it is a social post, an email sequence, a flyer, or a pitch deck — starts here as a brief. The team knows what is running, what is coming, and what needs to be built.

If it is not in the Marketing Engine, the team cannot support it.

---

## The Campaign Stages

| Stage | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Draft** | Idea captured — not yet approved or started |
| **Active** | Campaign is live and running |
| **Paused** | Temporarily on hold |
| **Completed** | Campaign has finished |

---

## How To Create a Campaign

1. Go to Dashboard → scroll to **Marketing Engine**
2. Click **+ New Campaign**
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Campaign Name** — what you are calling it (required)
   - **Type** — Social / Email / Event / Outreach / Content / Other
   - **Target Audience** — who this campaign is aimed at
   - **Goal** — what success looks like in one sentence
   - **Channel** — where it will run: Instagram / LinkedIn / Telegram / Email / In-Person
   - **Start Date** — when it begins
   - **End Date** — when it finishes (optional for ongoing campaigns)
4. Click **Save**
5. The campaign card appears in **Draft** stage

---

## How To Generate an AI Brief

Once a campaign is created you can use the built-in AI to generate a full campaign brief.

1. Open the campaign card
2. Click **Generate Brief**
3. The AI will produce:
   - A campaign summary
   - Suggested messaging and tone
   - Content ideas for the chosen channel
   - A simple action checklist
4. Review the brief — edit anything that does not fit
5. The brief saves automatically to the campaign card

> **The brief is a starting point, not a finished product.** Always review it with your team before executing. The AI does not know your relationships, your history, or your voice — you do.

---

## How To Move a Campaign Forward

As a campaign progresses, update its stage:

**Option 1 — Click the Status Badge**
- Click the stage badge on the campaign card (e.g. "Draft")
- It cycles forward to the next stage automatically

**Option 2 — Edit the Campaign**
- Open the card
- Click the **edit icon**
- Change the stage manually and save

---

## How To Edit or Delete a Campaign

**Edit:**
1. Open the campaign card
2. Click the **edit icon**
3. Update details and save

**Delete:**
1. Open the campaign card
2. Click the **trash icon**
3. Confirm the deletion

> **Warning:** Deletions are permanent. There is no undo.

---

## What Good Looks Like

- Every campaign idea is captured in Draft the day it is discussed — not held in someone's head
- Every active campaign has a clear goal written on the card
- AI briefs are reviewed and personalised before being handed to the team
- Completed campaigns are marked as Completed — not left sitting in Active
- The team can look at the Marketing Engine at any point and know exactly what is running

---

## What To Automate Later

Once you have run campaigns manually for a few weeks, these steps are candidates for automation:

- Campaign briefs auto-generated when a new card is created
- Scheduled reminders when a campaign start date is approaching
- Social posts queued directly from the campaign card to the Social Calendar
- Completion reports auto-generated when a campaign is marked as Completed

You will know it is time to automate when you find yourself rebuilding the same brief structure from scratch every single time.
