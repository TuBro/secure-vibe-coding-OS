# 04 — Sales Pipeline

**Section:** Sales  
**Tool:** Leads Kanban Board  
**Location:** Dashboard → scroll to Sales Pipeline

---

> **Remember:** You are doing this manually first so you understand it fully. Once you can run this step without thinking, that is when we automate it.

---

## Purpose

The Sales Pipeline tracks every prospect from the moment they show interest through to a closed deal. It gives the whole team a live view of where every lead sits at any point in time.

No lead should exist in your head or in a private chat. If it is not in the pipeline, it does not exist.

---

## The Four Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **New** | Lead has been identified — no contact made yet |
| **Contacted** | First outreach has been made — call, message, or email |
| **Qualified** | Lead has shown genuine interest — worth pursuing |
| **Closed** | Deal done or lead no longer active |

---

## How To Add a Lead

1. Go to Dashboard → scroll to **Sales Pipeline**
2. Click **+ Add Lead**
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Name** — full name of the contact (required)
   - **Company** — business name (optional but recommended)
   - **Phone** — best contact number (optional)
   - **Source** — where the lead came from: Telegram / Referral / Website / Cold
4. Click **Add Lead**
5. The card appears in the **New** column instantly

---

## How To Move a Lead Forward

As you make progress with a lead, move their card to the next column:

**Option 1 — Drag and Drop**
- Click and hold the lead card
- Drag it across to the next column
- Release

**Option 2 — Click the Status Badge**
- Click the status badge on the card (e.g. "New")
- It cycles forward automatically to the next stage

---

## How To Delete a Lead

1. Find the lead card
2. Click the **trash icon** on the card
3. Confirm the deletion

> **Warning:** Deletions are permanent. There is no undo.

---

## What Good Looks Like

- Every lead you hear about goes into the pipeline the same day
- No lead sits in **New** for more than 48 hours without being contacted
- **Qualified** column is reviewed every Monday morning
- **Closed** column is reviewed monthly — wins are celebrated, losses are learned from

---

## What To Automate Later

Once you have run this manually for a few weeks, these steps are candidates for automation:

- Leads auto-created when someone messages the Telegram bot
- Automatic reminder when a lead has not moved in 3 days
- Notification to the team when a lead reaches Qualified

You will know it is time to automate when you find yourself doing the same manual step every single day without thinking.
