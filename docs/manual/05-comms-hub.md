# 05 — Communications Hub

**Section:** Communications  
**Tool:** Unified Outreach Log  
**Location:** Dashboard → scroll to Communications Hub

---

> **Remember:** You are doing this manually first so you understand it fully. Once you can run this step without thinking, that is when we automate it.

---

## Purpose

The Communications Hub is the single record of every outreach your team makes — calls, messages, emails, and Telegram contacts — all in one place.

If you spoke to someone, messaged someone, or emailed someone about business, it goes here.

No conversation should live only in your phone or your memory. If it is not logged, it did not happen.

---

## The Communication Types

| Type | When To Use |
|------|-------------|
| **Email** | Any email sent to a lead, client, or partner |
| **Telegram** | Any message or voice note sent via Telegram |
| **WhatsApp** | Any WhatsApp message sent for business purposes |
| **Call** | Any phone or video call made |
| **Meeting** | Any in-person or scheduled meeting |

---

## How To Log a Communication

1. Go to Dashboard → scroll to **Communications Hub**
2. Click **+ Log Communication**
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Contact Name** — who you reached out to (required)
   - **Type** — Email / Telegram / WhatsApp / Call / Meeting
   - **Direction** — Outbound (you reached out) or Inbound (they reached out to you)
   - **Notes** — a one or two sentence summary of what was discussed or sent
   - **Linked Lead** — connect this communication to a lead in the Sales Pipeline (optional but recommended)
4. Click **Save**
5. The entry appears in the Communications log instantly

---

## How To Review the Log

- Entries are listed in reverse chronological order — newest first
- Use the **Type filter** to view only calls, or only emails, etc.
- Click any entry to expand it and read the full notes
- Linked leads show as a badge — click to go directly to that lead card

---

## How To Edit or Delete an Entry

**Edit:**
1. Click the entry to expand it
2. Click the **edit icon**
3. Update the details and save

**Delete:**
1. Click the entry to expand it
2. Click the **trash icon**
3. Confirm the deletion

> **Warning:** Deletions are permanent. There is no undo.

---

## What Good Looks Like

- Every outreach is logged the same day it happens — not at the end of the week
- Notes are brief but clear — enough that anyone on the team understands what was said without asking you
- Every communication linked to a lead in the Sales Pipeline
- Inbound messages logged just as consistently as outbound ones
- No lead in the pipeline without at least one communication entry

---

## What To Automate Later

Once you have logged communications manually for a few weeks, these steps are candidates for automation:

- Telegram messages auto-logged when sent through Te Ra
- Inbound Telegram voice notes create a communication entry automatically
- Email replies from tracked leads auto-logged via email integration
- Reminder triggered when a qualified lead has had no communication in 5 days

You will know it is time to automate when you find yourself copying and pasting the same notes every single day.
