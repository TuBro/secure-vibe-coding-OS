# 06 — Support Tickets

**Section:** Support  
**Tool:** Ticket Tracker  
**Location:** Dashboard → scroll to Support Tickets

---

> **Remember:** You are doing this manually first so you understand it fully. Once you can run this step without thinking, that is when we automate it.

---

## Purpose

The Support Ticket system is where every client issue, internal problem, and follow-up task gets recorded and resolved.

If a client reports something broken, a team member flags a problem, or something needs to be actioned — it goes here as a ticket. Nothing gets lost in a chat thread or forgotten in someone's inbox.

If it is not a ticket, it is not being tracked.

---

## The Ticket Stages

| Stage | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Open** | Ticket raised — no one has started working on it yet |
| **In Progress** | Someone is actively working on it |
| **Waiting** | Blocked — waiting on a client, a third party, or more information |
| **Resolved** | Issue is fixed and confirmed |
| **Closed** | Ticket archived — resolution verified by the person who raised it |

---

## The Priority Levels

| Priority | When To Use |
|----------|-------------|
| **Low** | Minor issue — no urgency, can wait |
| **Medium** | Standard issue — should be resolved within a few days |
| **High** | Significant impact — needs attention today |
| **Critical** | Everything stops — this blocks the business or a client |

---

## How To Raise a Ticket

1. Go to Dashboard → scroll to **Support Tickets**
2. Click **+ New Ticket**
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Title** — one clear sentence describing the problem (required)
   - **Description** — what happened, when, and what was expected instead
   - **Priority** — Low / Medium / High / Critical
   - **Type** — Client Issue / Internal / Bug / Task / Question
   - **Assigned To** — who is responsible for resolving it (optional)
   - **Linked Lead** — connect to a Sales Pipeline lead if relevant (optional)
4. Click **Save**
5. The ticket appears in **Open** immediately

---

## How To Write a Good Ticket Title

A good title tells anyone on the team exactly what the problem is at a glance.

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| "Website broken" | "Contact form not sending emails on mobile" |
| "Client unhappy" | "Tu80 client — invoice not received for March" |
| "Fix this" | "Dashboard not loading for new user after sign-up" |

One sentence. Specific. Actionable.

---

## How To Move a Ticket Forward

As work progresses, update the ticket stage:

**Option 1 — Click the Status Badge**
- Click the stage badge on the ticket (e.g. "Open")
- It cycles to the next stage automatically

**Option 2 — Edit the Ticket**
- Open the ticket
- Click the **edit icon**
- Change the stage manually and save

---

## How To Edit or Delete a Ticket

**Edit:**
1. Open the ticket
2. Click the **edit icon**
3. Update details and save

**Delete:**
1. Open the ticket
2. Click the **trash icon**
3. Confirm the deletion

> **Warning:** Deletions are permanent. There is no undo.

---

## What Good Looks Like

- Every issue raised — by a client or the team — is in the system as a ticket within the hour
- Every ticket has a clear title and enough description that someone else can pick it up without asking questions
- No ticket sits in **Open** for more than 24 hours without being assigned
- **Critical** tickets are picked up immediately — not at the next standup
- **Resolved** tickets are confirmed closed by the person who raised them
- The team reviews all **Open** and **In Progress** tickets every Monday morning

---

## What To Automate Later

Once you have run tickets manually for a few weeks, these steps are candidates for automation:

- Tickets auto-created when a client sends a specific message via Telegram
- Escalation alert when a High or Critical ticket has had no activity for 2 hours
- Weekly digest of open tickets sent to the team every Monday morning
- Resolved tickets automatically prompt the raiser to confirm and close

You will know it is time to automate when you find yourself raising the same type of ticket from the same source every single week.
