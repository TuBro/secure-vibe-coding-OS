# 07 — Social Calendar

**Section:** Social Media & Bookings  
**Tool:** Content Queue and Calendar  
**Location:** Dashboard → scroll to Social Calendar

---

> **Remember:** You are doing this manually first so you understand it fully. Once you can run this step without thinking, that is when we automate it.

---

## Purpose

The Social Calendar is where all content posts, bookings, and scheduled meetings are managed in one place.

If you are posting to Instagram, scheduling a client meeting, or queuing a LinkedIn article — it goes here. The team can see what is planned, what is live, and what is coming up without having to ask anyone.

If it is not on the calendar, the team does not know it is happening.

---

## The Two Types of Entries

| Type | What It Is |
|------|------------|
| **Content Post** | A social media post planned for a specific date and platform |
| **Booking** | A meeting, call, or event scheduled with a client or partner |

---

## Content Post — Platforms

| Platform | Use For |
|----------|---------|
| **Instagram** | Visual content, brand presence, community |
| **LinkedIn** | Professional updates, thought leadership, B2B outreach |
| **Facebook** | Community groups, events, local reach |
| **Telegram** | Direct channel announcements to followers |
| **TikTok** | Short video content, reach and awareness |

---

## The Content Post Stages

| Stage | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Idea** | Post concept captured — not yet written or designed |
| **In Production** | Copy or creative is being built |
| **Ready** | Post is finished and approved — waiting for publish date |
| **Published** | Post is live |

---

## How To Add a Content Post

1. Go to Dashboard → scroll to **Social Calendar**
2. Click **+ Add Post**
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Title** — a short description of what the post is about (required)
   - **Platform** — Instagram / LinkedIn / Facebook / Telegram / TikTok
   - **Scheduled Date** — when it will be published
   - **Caption / Copy** — the text of the post (optional at idea stage)
   - **Stage** — Idea / In Production / Ready / Published
   - **Linked Campaign** — connect to a Marketing Engine campaign if relevant (optional)
4. Click **Save**
5. The post appears on the calendar on the scheduled date

---

## How To Add a Booking

1. Go to Dashboard → scroll to **Social Calendar**
2. Click **+ Add Booking**
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Title** — who you are meeting and why (required)
   - **Date and Time** — when it is scheduled
   - **Location** — in-person address, Zoom link, or phone call
   - **Contact Name** — who you are meeting
   - **Notes** — anything the team needs to know before the meeting
4. Click **Save**
5. The booking appears on the calendar on the scheduled date

---

## How To Move a Post Forward

As content progresses through production, update its stage:

**Option 1 — Click the Status Badge**
- Click the stage badge on the post card (e.g. "Idea")
- It cycles forward to the next stage automatically

**Option 2 — Edit the Post**
- Open the post card
- Click the **edit icon**
- Change the stage manually and save

---

## How To Edit or Delete an Entry

**Edit:**
1. Open the post or booking card
2. Click the **edit icon**
3. Update details and save

**Delete:**
1. Open the post or booking card
2. Click the **trash icon**
3. Confirm the deletion

> **Warning:** Deletions are permanent. There is no undo.

---

## What Good Looks Like

- The calendar always has at least two weeks of content planned ahead
- No post sits in **Idea** for more than a week without moving to In Production
- Every booking has a contact name and clear notes so any team member can step in if needed
- Posts are linked to their campaign in the Marketing Engine
- The team reviews the Social Calendar every Monday to confirm the week ahead is ready

---

## What To Automate Later

Once you have run the calendar manually for a few weeks, these steps are candidates for automation:

- Posts auto-published to social platforms on their scheduled date
- Booking reminders sent to the team 24 hours before a meeting
- Content ideas auto-generated from active Marketing Engine campaigns
- Weekly calendar summary sent to the team every Sunday night

You will know it is time to automate when you find yourself manually copying the same post to the same platform at the same time every week.
