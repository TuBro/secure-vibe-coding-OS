# 09 — Handover & Audit

**Section:** Delivery  
**Tool:** Task Board — Handover Checklist  
**Location:** Dashboard → Task Board

---

> **Remember:** Nothing is handed over until it has been tested. Internal first. Client second. Both sign off.

---

## Purpose

The Handover Audit is the final quality check before a system is delivered to a client. It protects the team from handing over something broken and protects the client from receiving something untested.

Two stages. Both are required. Neither can be skipped.

---

## Stage 1 — Internal Audit

The team runs through every item on the checklist before the client sees anything. Issues found here are fixed quietly. Only when every item passes does the system move to Stage 2.

**Who runs it:** The operator or team lead  
**When:** Before any client demonstration or delivery  
**Standard:** Every item must pass — not most, not almost all. Every item.

---

## Stage 2 — Client Approved Handover Audit

The client runs through the same checklist with the operator present. The client tests each item with their own hands. When every item passes, the client signs off.

This signature closes the delivery. It is the record that the system was tested, accepted, and handed over in working condition.

**Who runs it:** The client, with the operator present  
**When:** At the delivery session  
**Standard:** Every item must pass before the client signs

---

## The 19-Point Checklist

### Authentication
- [ ] Sign up with a new account
- [ ] Sign in and sign out

### Voice Notes & Te Ra
- [ ] Send a voice note via Telegram — confirm it appears in the Voice Activity Feed
- [ ] Confirm Te Ra replies by voice

### Task Board
- [ ] Add a new task manually
- [ ] Drag a task through To Do → In Progress → Done
- [ ] Delete a task

### Sales Pipeline
- [ ] Add a new lead
- [ ] Move a lead by drag and drop
- [ ] Move a lead by clicking the status badge

### Communications Hub
- [ ] Log a new communication and link it to a lead

### Marketing Engine
- [ ] Create a campaign and generate an AI brief
- [ ] Move a campaign through stages

### Support Tickets
- [ ] Create a ticket with High priority
- [ ] Move a ticket through stages

### Social Calendar
- [ ] Add a content post and a booking

### System & Admin
- [ ] Confirm token usage displays and services show Online

### General
- [ ] Check dashboard loads on mobile
- [ ] Confirm dark mode works across all sections
