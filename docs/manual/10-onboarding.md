# 10 — Onboarding

**Section:** Onboarding  
**Tool:** All sections  
**Location:** Dashboard → all sections

---

> **Remember:** A new member does not get full access until the Agreement is signed. Onboarding begins after signing, not before.

---

## Purpose

Onboarding is the process of bringing a new Vaka Tu80 Core member into the system. It covers access setup, system orientation, first steps, and confirmation that the member is operational.

A well-onboarded member can run the system independently within their first week.

---

## Who This Applies To

Any person who has:
1. Signed the Vaka Tu80 Core Agreement (Section 11)
2. Been granted access by the operator
3. Been assigned a role within the organisation

---

## Step 1 — Before Their First Login

The operator completes these steps before the new member touches the system:

- [ ] Create their account in Clerk (or send them the sign-up link)
- [ ] Confirm their email address is registered
- [ ] Set their access level — full access or restricted
- [ ] Add them to the Telegram group
- [ ] Introduce them to Te Ra on Telegram

---

## Step 2 — First Login

The new member completes these steps on their first session:

1. Go to **https://secure-vibe-coding-os-three.vercel.app**
2. Click **Sign Up** and create their account using their team email
3. Sign in and confirm the dashboard loads
4. Send a test voice note to Te Ra on Telegram
5. Confirm the voice note appears in the Voice Activity Feed on the dashboard

If any of these steps fail — stop and contact the operator before proceeding.

---

## Step 3 — System Orientation

The operator walks the new member through each section of the manual in order:

| Session | Sections to Cover |
|---------|------------------|
| **Day 1** | 01 Task Board, 02 Voice Notes & Te Ra |
| **Day 2** | 03 Marketing Engine, 04 Sales Pipeline |
| **Day 3** | 05 Communications Hub, 06 Support Tickets |
| **Day 4** | 07 Social Calendar, 08 System & Admin |

Each session should take no more than 30 minutes. The member reads the manual section first, then practices on the live system with the operator present.

---

## Step 4 — First Week Tasks

By the end of their first week the new member should have:

- [ ] Added at least one lead to the Sales Pipeline
- [ ] Logged at least one communication in the Communications Hub
- [ ] Created at least one task on the Task Board and moved it to Done
- [ ] Sent at least three voice notes to Te Ra
- [ ] Read all 8 sections of the operations manual

---

At the end of the first week the operator reviews the member's activity and confirms they are operational.

---

## What Good Looks Like

- The new member is using the system independently by day 5
- They are sending voice notes to Te Ra without being reminded
- They are logging their work on the Task Board without being asked
- They ask questions about process, not about how to use the system
