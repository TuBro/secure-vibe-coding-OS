# 11 — Agreements & Signing

**Section:** Agreements  
**Tool:** Manual process — digital or physical signature  
**Location:** Completed before access is granted

---

> **Note:** This is the manual process for now. This document is designed to be executed by smart contract on-chain in a future phase. The structure here is the same structure the smart contract will follow — nothing will need to be rewritten, only automated.

---

## Purpose

The Agreement is the formal record that a Vaka Tu80 Core member has understood, accepted, and committed to the kaupapa before access is granted.

No agreement. No access. No exceptions.

---

## Why We Sign Before We Onboard

Signing first is not a formality — it is a standard. It establishes:

1. **Shared understanding** — the member knows what they are joining and why
2. **Mutual commitment** — both sides have made a declaration
3. **Legal protection** — the record exists if the relationship changes
4. **Immutable record** — when this moves to blockchain, the signature is permanent and cannot be disputed

The agreement is not a barrier. It is an alignment check. If someone is not willing to sign, they are not aligned to the destination.

---

## The Vaka Tu80 Core Member Agreement

### What the Member Is Agreeing To

By signing this agreement the member confirms they:

1. **Understand the kaupapa** — they have read the purpose of Vaka Tu80 Core and share the same destination
2. **Will operate with integrity** — they will use the system and its data honestly and in service of the mission
3. **Will protect confidentiality** — all business data, client information, and internal operations remain confidential
4. **Will follow the manual** — they will operate the system as documented and not work around established processes
5. **Accept their responsibilities** — they understand their role and will perform it to the standard expected
6. **Acknowledge the technology** — they understand the system is AI-assisted and that human judgement remains primary

## After Signing

Once the agreement is signed:

1. File the signed copy — digital or physical
2. Proceed to [10 — Onboarding](10-onboarding.md)
3. Grant system access only after Step 1 of onboarding is complete

---

## Future State — Smart Contract Execution

When this process moves on-chain:

- The agreement will be a smart contract deployed on a blockchain
- The member signs with their wallet — the signature is cryptographically verified
- The contract executes automatically — access is granted the moment both parties sign
- The record is permanent and immutable — it cannot be altered or disputed
- No operator needs to be present — the contract enforces the process

The manual process you follow today is the exact logic the smart contract will execute tomorrow.
